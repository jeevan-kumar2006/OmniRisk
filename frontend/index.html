Data Quality vs Data Relevance Separation

Data Quality: Measures intrinsic record validity. Claims occurring outside policy boundaries or exceeding coverage sums are cataloged and segregated rather than silently deleted.

Data Relevance: Measures the volume of logically sound historical information available to support individual risk formulas. Accounts with high record integrity achieve 100% feature support.

AI Governance & Responsible Underwriting Architecture

Compliance, transparency, and guardrail verification checklist.

🔒

PII Redaction

FirstName, LastName, and DOB are stripped during ingestion. Age bands are derived before DOB removal.

⚖️

Authoritative Score

The deterministic engine is the sole source of truth for numeric scoring (0-100). GenAI cannot modify scores.

🔍

Evidence Traceability

Every risk driver is linked to verifiable historical claim and payment records. Hallucinations are barred.

👤

Human Oversight

All AI outputs are framed as decision-support guidance for human underwriters. Autonomous binding is disallowed.

🔄

Zero-Failure Fallback

If remote GenAI credentials are unavailable or offline, the verified deterministic synthesis engine activates.

🛡️

Prompt Injection Defense

Customer data is treated as untrusted data. Input fields are sanitized to prevent directive overrides.
